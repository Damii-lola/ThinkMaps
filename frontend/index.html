Sample idea card — illustrative

Shiftwell

Recovery and sleep tracking built around rotating shifts, not a fixed 9-to-5.

Core problem

Existing fitness apps assume a regular daytime schedule. Shift workers fall out of every streak, every reminder, every "morning routine" — the app is fighting their clock instead of reading it.

10x upgrade

A schedule-aware engine that re-times reminders, fasting windows, and sleep targets around the next shift, not the calendar date.

Monetization

Free tracking, paid schedule-sync with wearables and employer roster imports — tested at $6.99 and $9.99.
